What we do

The services of
your department

We cover every administrative service your business needs as one integrated team. Each service has a clear scope, so you get consistent quality and measurable results.

🧾
Electronic Invoicing (GTI)
Issuing, sending and recording electronic invoices in line with Costa Rica's Ministry of Finance (Hacienda) system.
💰
Active Collections
Systematic follow-up on receivables, contact with late payers and status reports on accounts receivable.
📋
CCSS and INS Payroll Filings
Monthly employer filings with CCSS (social security) through SICERE and INS (workers' compensation) policies, accurate and on time.
👥
Salary Payroll Management
Calculation of salaries, deductions, aguinaldo (mandatory year-end bonus) and severance for your team.
📊
CRM and Quotes
Keeping your CRM up to date, following up on opportunities and preparing formal quotes.
🗓️
Calendar and Email Management
Organizing your calendar, coordinating appointments and handling administrative email.
🏦
Accounts Payable
Control and scheduling of supplier payments to avoid late fees and keep business relationships healthy.
📁
Government Paperwork and Forms
Handling procedures with public institutions, completing forms and following up on filings.
📈
Operational Reports
Monthly reports on cash flow, operational status and key metrics to support your decisions.
